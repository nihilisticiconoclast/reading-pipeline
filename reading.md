# Reading

- **The Looking Glass War** — John le Carré (1965). A failed operation dissected with le Carré's coldest precision; the most unsentimental of the Circus novels.
- **The Valley of Fear** — Arthur Conan Doyle (1915). The last Holmes novel; the Birlstone puzzle is strong, the American backstory less so.
- **Moral AI: And How We Get There** — Jana Schaich Borg (2023). AI ethics grounded in cognitive science rather than philosophy; more empirical than most entries in the field.
- **The Ecology of Wisdom** — Arne Naess (2008). Selected writings from the founder of deep ecology; self-realisation extended to encompass all living things.
